# GroceryAssistant Support

Thank you for using GroceryAssistant.

If you have any questions, suggestions, or encounter any issues while using the app, please contact us:

Email: [support@example.com](mailto:support@example.com)

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I add a grocery item?
Tap the "+" button and enter the item name.

### How do I mark an item as completed?
Tap the checkbox next to the item.

### How do I report a bug?
Please send us an email with details about the issue and your device model.


## Feedback
We welcome your feedback and feature suggestions to help improve GroceryAssistant.
